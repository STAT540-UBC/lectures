
# Biological network

## 1. How do we measure gene/protein interactions?

### A. Yeast two-hybrid (Y2H) — binary physical interactions

- **Fields & Song** (1989) [*Nature* 340:245-246](https://www.nature.com/articles/340245a0). Invented the yeast two-hybrid system.

### B. Affinity purification–mass spectrometry (AP-MS) — protein complexes

- **Gavin et al.** (2002) [*Nature* 415:141-147](https://www.nature.com/articles/415141a). First proteome-scale AP-MS (yeast TAP-MS).

### C. Proximity labeling — interactions in living cells

- **Roux et al.** (2012) [*J Cell Biol* 196:801-810](https://rupress.org/jcb/article/196/6/801/37564). Original BioID paper — introduced proximity-dependent biotinylation.

### D. Genetic interaction networks — synthetic lethality

- **Costanzo et al.** (2016) [*Science* 353:aaf1420](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aaf1420). Global yeast genetic interaction network (~23M double-mutant combinations).

### Further reading

- **Uetz et al.** (2000) [*Nature* 403:623-627](https://www.nature.com/articles/35001009). First genome-wide Y2H screen (yeast).
- **Luck et al.** (2020) [*Nature* 580:402-408](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-020-2188-x). HuRI: ~53K human binary PPIs.
- **Huttlin et al.** (2015) [*Cell* 162:425-440](https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(15)00768-1). BioPlex: first large-scale human AP-MS interactome.
- **Qin et al.** (2021) [*Nature Methods* 18:133-143](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-020-01010-5). Review of BioID, TurboID, APEX.
- **Kaelin** (2005) [*Nat Rev Cancer* 5:689-698](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrc1691). The concept of synthetic lethality in anticancer therapy.

## 2. Network analysis in action

### A. Network as a tool to gather scattered evidence (network propagation)

- **Cowen et al.** (2017) [*Nat Rev Genet* 18:551-562](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrg.2017.38). Review: network propagation as a universal amplifier of genetic associations.

### B. Network to identify groups and pathways (community detection)

- **Menche et al.** (2015) [*Science* 347:1257601](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1257601). Disease genes form modules; module overlap predicts comorbidity.

### C. Network to model gene regulatory programs

- **Aibar et al.** (2017) [*Nat Methods* 14:1083-1086](https://www.nature.com/articles/nmeth.4463). SCENIC: GRN inference from scRNA-seq.

### Further reading

- **Leiserson et al.** (2015) [*Nat Genet* 47:106-114](https://www.nature.com/articles/ng.3168). HotNet2: heat diffusion to find mutated subnetworks across cancers.
- **Hofree et al.** (2013) [*Nat Methods* 10:1108-1115](https://www.nature.com/articles/nmeth.2651). NBS: project mutations onto PPI network, cluster to find cancer subtypes.
- **Choobdar et al.** (2019) [*Nat Methods* 16:843-852](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-019-0509-5). DREAM challenge benchmarking 75 module identification methods.
- **Margolin et al.** (2006) [*BMC Bioinformatics* 7:S7](https://bmcbioinformatics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/1471-2105-7-S1-S7). ARACNE: information-theoretic GRN reconstruction.



## 3. Cell-cell communication networks

### A. Ligand-receptor signaling foundations

- **Ramilowski et al.** (2015) [*Nat Commun* 6:7866](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms8866). FANTOM5 L-R connectome: 1,894 ligand-receptor pairs across 144 human cell types. The foundational catalog.

### B. Cell-cell communication in tissue context

- **Vento-Tormo et al.** (2018) [*Nature* 563:347-353](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-018-0698-6). Single-cell atlas of the maternal-fetal interface — mapped L-R interactions between immune, stromal, and trophoblast cells in the decidua.

### Further reading

- **Armingol et al.** (2021) [*Nat Rev Genet* 22:71-88](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41576-020-00292-x). Definitive review of cell-cell communication biology and how we study it.
