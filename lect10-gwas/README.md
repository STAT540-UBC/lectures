# Lecture 10. Genome-Wide Association Studies

## Prereading

1. **Altshuler, D., Daly, M. J., & Lander, E. S. (2008).** [Genetic mapping in human disease](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1156409). *Science*, 322(5903), 881-888.
   - Foundational paper discussing the intellectual foundations of genetic mapping for both Mendelian and complex traits, with insights from linkage analysis and early GWAS.

2. **Visscher, P. M., Wray, N. R., Zhang, Q., Sklar, P., McCarthy, M. I., Brown, M. A., & Yang, J. (2017).** [10 Years of GWAS Discovery: Biology, Function, and Translation](https://www.cell.com/ajhg/fulltext/S0002-9297(17)30240-9). *American Journal of Human Genetics*, 101(1), 5-22.
   - Comprehensive review of GWAS discoveries over the first decade, covering population genetics, disease biology, and therapeutic applications.

3. **Visscher, P. M., & Goddard, M. E. (2019).** [From R.A. Fisher's 1918 Paper to GWAS a Century Later](https://academic.oup.com/genetics/article/211/4/1125/5931511). *Genetics*, 211(4), 1125-1130.
   - Historical perspective connecting Fisher's foundational work on quantitative genetics to modern genome-wide association studies.
